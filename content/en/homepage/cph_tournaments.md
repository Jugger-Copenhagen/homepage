This year we're excited to announce our second Jugger tournament! Come and experience a weekend of fast-paced action, laughter, and community spirit as teams from Germany and Austria come to compete in this unique sport that combines rugby, dodgeball, and pure chaos. Never seen Jugger before? Perfect—this is your chance to discover why players get so hooked on this thrilling game.

- Where: [DTU, grass field at Henrik Dams Allé](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WnpEsabu1vNzGkCf9)
- When: Saturday & Sunday, 8-9 August 2026, 10:00-17:00

So grab a friend, bring some energy, and join us for an unforgettable weekend.
[Learn more](https://turniere.jugger.org/tournament.php?id=913).
